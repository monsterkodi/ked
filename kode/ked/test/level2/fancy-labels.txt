fancy-labels -- hidden characters inside the visible label

range    U+E0020 .. U+E007E, no U+E007F

every line shows a codepoint and nothing else. the hidden characters are
interleaved with the label itself, so an editor that miscounts them breaks
the label, not a decoration at the end of the line.

rule     "U+E" is wrapped in one random special character
         a non-zero digit is wrapped in n special characters, n = digit
         a zero, and a hex letter A..F, is not wrapped

with ? standing for any special character:

  ?U+E?00??2??0           U+E0020
  ?U+E?00??2???1?         U+E0021

the wrappers are random per occurrence, from U+E0020..U+E007E.
